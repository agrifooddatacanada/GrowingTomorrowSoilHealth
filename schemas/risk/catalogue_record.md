## Description

The Agri-Environmental Indicator Soil Erosion Risk dataset provides an estimated risk of soil loss from the combined effects of wind, water, and tillage for Soil Landscapes of Canada agricultural areas.  

Products in this data series present results for predefined areas as defined by the Soil Landscapes of Canada (SLC v.3.2) data series, uniquely identified by SOIL_LANDSCAPE_ID values.  

Data is provided in two forms:  semi-decadal results include only the publication years for the Census of Agriculture since 1981; and annual results include every year since 1981.  Semi-decadal results form the backbone of trend analysis, whilst the annual results estimate results between Census of Agriculture years and forecasts beyond the most recent Census of Agriculture.  Additionally, the annual results respect provincial boundaries which can be of use when analyzing results per province. 

Purpose:  These datasets provide an estimate of the level of erosion risk for Canadian agricultural lands, given actual farm practices. They are useful for creating maps and for policy analysis. For example, they can be used to evaluate the impact of changing land use practices on soil erosion risk, or to identify regions of concern.
